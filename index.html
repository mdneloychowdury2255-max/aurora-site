The craft rules

Four constraints that keep this small enough to trust.
01

One file, always

Every page ships as a single HTML document. If it needs a build step to exist, it is too heavy to be honest.

02

Motion earns its place

Movement marks state and attention. Anything that loops forever without a reason gets cut.

03

Phone first, always

Contrast, tap targets and line length are designed on a small screen, then widened — never the reverse.

04

Nothing phones home

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts. What happens on the bench stays on the device.

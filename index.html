Four habits

The rules that keep this small enough to trust.
01

Ship one file

Every page is a single HTML document. If it needs a build step to exist, it is too heavy to be honest.

02

Motion means something

Movement marks state and attention. Anything that loops forever without a reason gets removed.

03

Phone before desktop

Contrast, tap targets and line length are designed on a small screen, then widened — never the reverse.

04

Nothing phones home

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts. What happens in the lab stays on the device.

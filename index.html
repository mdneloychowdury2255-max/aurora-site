What we build

Four things we do well, and refuse to compromise on.

01

One-file sites

Every page ships as a single HTML document with inline CSS and JS. Nothing to install, nothing to break, trivial to host anywhere.

02

Motion with purpose

Animation marks state and attention. If a loop runs forever without a reason, it gets cut before it ships.

03

Phone-first design

Contrast, tap targets and line length are solved on a small screen first, then widened — never the other way round.

04

Nothing phones home

No analytics, no cookies, no third-party scripts. What happens on the page stays on the device.

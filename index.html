FAQ

Questions, answered.

The things most people ask before booking.
How long does a project take?

Most one-page projects go live within five working days of the call. Launch packages with emails usually take seven to ten. If you have a hard deadline, say so on the call and we will confirm before starting.

Do you use templates or page builders?

No. Every page is designed and built by hand as a single lightweight file. It loads fast, works on any phone and has nothing running in the background that you did not ask for.

What do you need from me to start?

A short call, whatever you already have (deck, old site, notes) and access to your domain when it is time to go live. You do not need to prepare anything formal.

What if I want changes after launch?

Every package includes a set number of revision rounds plus thirty days of small fixes. After that, follow-up work is billed hourly at a rate we agree on up front.

How does payment work?

Half before we start, half when the page goes live. You get a written scope and a fixed price before anything is owed.
